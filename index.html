Privacy Policy — Ludo Sabha

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Last updated: 24 November 2026

App package: com.aistudio.ludosabha.rkvxnm

This Privacy Policy explains how the Ludo Sabha Android app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. The App is a Ludo board game that you can play offline against computer bots, online with friends in private rooms, or online with other players through public matchmaking, with optional real-time voice chat.

In short
  • No sign-up, login or account is needed. We do not ask for your email, phone number, contacts or location.
  • We do not run a user database. Online rooms exist only while a game is in progress.
  • Voice chat is optional, works only while you keep the microphone on, and is never recorded.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers as described below.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

a) Information you provide

  • Display name (guest name): You may enter a nickname. It is saved on your device and is sent to the game server only to show it to the other players in your room. You do not have to use your real name.
  • Settings: Your language, sound and voice volume, dice theme, bot difficulty and turn-timer choices are stored only on your device.

b) Online multiplayer data

When you create or join an online room, or use public matchmaking to be matched with other online players, the App connects to our game server and exchanges the data needed to run the match: the room code, your display name, a temporary player identifier, your game moves and dice results, and quick-reply emoji reactions you send. This data is kept in server memory only for the duration of the room and is not saved to a database. Like any internet service, the server necessarily receives your IP address to deliver the connection; we do not use it to identify you and do not store it in a user profile.

c) Blocking and reporting other players

  • Block: You can block another player in a match. Blocking also mutes that player's voice for you. Your block list is kept only on your device.
  • Report: You can report a player for abusive behaviour. A report sends the room code, your temporary player identifier, the reported player's temporary identifier and the reason you select to our game server so that abuse can be addressed. Reports do not include voice recordings, because voice is never recorded.

d) Microphone and voice chat

  • The App requests the Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) permission only for the optional in-game voice chat. You can deny it and still play.
  • Audio is captured only while you have joined voice chat and your microphone is unmuted. It is streamed live and encrypted to the other players in your room through the LiveKit real-time audio service.
  • Voice is not recorded, stored, transcribed or analysed by us.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT) and audio-settings permissions are used only to route game sound and voice chat to your headphones or Bluetooth headset. The App does not scan for or track nearby devices.

e) Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and, where permitted, personalise ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating-system version, app information and ad interactions. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the App's privacy options.

See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

f) Data stored only on your device

Match statistics, your preferences, your block list and your display name are saved locally on your phone. They are not uploaded to us. Android cloud backup is disabled for this App.

g) What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise or approximate location, camera data or payment information. The App has no in-app purchases and no real-money gaming.

3. Data retention and deletion

  • Online room data is discarded when the room ends or all players leave.
  • Abuse reports are kept only as long as needed to review them and act on abuse.
  • Voice audio is not retained.
  • Local data can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's storage (Settings → Apps → Ludo Sabha → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the App.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device's Google settings.

Because we do not keep accounts or a user database, we usually hold no stored personal data about you. If you have a request, contact us using the details below.

4. Security

Connections to our game server and voice service are encrypted in transit. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we keep the data we handle to the minimum needed to run the game.

5. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your choices

  • Deny or revoke the microphone permission at any time in Android settings; you can still play without voice chat.
  • Mute your microphone or leave voice chat during a match, and mute, block or report any other player.
  • Play only with friends you invite by using a private room code instead of public matchmaking.
  • Use any nickname you like instead of your real name.
  • Change ad consent from the App's privacy options (where available) and manage ad personalisation in your device settings.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
